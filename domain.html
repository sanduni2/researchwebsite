Domain

Research Domain

Research focus: an integrated poultry intelligence framework where health, environment, feed, and egg quality continuously influence one another.

A comprehensive exploration of integrated AIoT solutions for poultry farming, covering health intelligence, environmental prediction, feed optimization, and egg quality traceability.

Domain Focus

Turn disconnected farm activities into one measurable and automated intelligence cycle.

Literature Survey

Existing research validates strong performance in isolated domains: poultry disease detection, environmental production prediction, feed analytics, and egg grading. However, previous systems usually operate as separate tools without real-time interconnection.

Research Gap

Existing solutions do not integrate multiple capabilities such as multi-modal disease detection, environmental-based production forecasting, cost-aware growth prediction, and automated egg grading in one system.

Research Problem

Disease outbreaks, environmental stress, feed inefficiency, and inconsistent egg quality control are interconnected problems. Treating them separately leads to delayed, incomplete, and costly responses.

Research Objectives

Build an Integrated Farm System (IFS) that continuously captures farm conditions, performs AI/ML inference, predicts outcomes, and triggers coordinated cross-domain actions through one unified platform.

Methodology

A multi-layer architecture combining IoT sensing, CNN-based vision models (MobileNetV2 and ResNet50), Random Forest prediction, Flask REST APIs, MongoDB data services, and Polygon-based blockchain traceability for egg batches.

Technologies Used

Python, Flask, React Native, MongoDB, REST APIs, Raspberry Pi, Jetson Nano, ESP32, CNN, MobileNetV2, ResNet50, Random Forest, Polygon blockchain, and end-to-end AIoT integration.

Research Pillars

Core Investigation Tracks

Pillar 01

Biological Health Intelligence

Early health-risk identification through multi-stage analysis of vocal signals, visual symptoms, and droppings classification.

Pillar 02

Environmental Performance Modeling

Five-parameter environmental sensing with production forecasting to quantify climate impact on laying performance.

Pillar 03

Nutrition and Growth Optimization

Feed-plate vision analytics and growth forecasting to adapt feed plans using real flock behavior data.

Pillar 04

Quality and Trust Layer

AI-driven egg grading with blockchain traceability to maintain transparent, auditable quality records.
